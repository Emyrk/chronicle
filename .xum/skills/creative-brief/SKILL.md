# Chronicle Creative Brief

## Game-Play Performance Analysis for Classic World of Warcraft

## **1. Product Overview**

**Chronicle** is a game-play performance analysis tool for **Classic World of Warcraft**, launching on the **Turtle WoW server**. It transforms complex raid logs into a **clear, accessible, and leadership-focused experience**, empowering raid leaders to coach more effectively and guilds to improve performance, efficiency, and accountability.

Unlike existing tools, Chronicle prioritizes **readability, contribution clarity, and missing performance context**, such as consumable investment and true raid impact. Chronicle is designed to grow in analytical depth over time while remaining approachable from day one.

## **2. Problem Statement**

Current log-analysis tools on Turtle WoW suffer from three core issues:

1. **High barrier to entry**  
   Interfaces are technically dense and difficult to interpret, limiting adoption and learning.  
2. **Incomplete data**  
   Key performance factors—such as consumable usage, resource investment, and contextual contribution—are not surfaced.  
3. **Poor leadership utility**  
   Tools focus on raw metrics rather than actionable insight, making raid coaching and attribution harder than necessary.

Chronicle exists to **reduce friction, expand insight, and support better leadership decisions**.

## **3. Target Audience**

### **Primary Audience**

**Raid Leaders**

* Responsible for coaching, strategy, and raid improvement  
  Need fast, interpretable insights  
* Value neutrality, accuracy, and efficiency over flashy parsing

### **Secondary Audience**

**Guild Masters**

* Use Chronicle as a public artifact for recruitment and progression  
* Share Chronicle links with raiders  
  Value transparency and accountability

### **Tertiary / Early Adopters**

* Hardcore players and edge-case communities (e.g., "level one lunatics")  
* Highly motivated, eager testers with no existing alternatives  
  Provide early feedback and stress-test data depth

**Important:** Individual players consume data but do not generate uploads. Upload authority belongs to leadership.

## **4. Product Goals**

### **User Outcomes**

* Raid leaders improve coaching quality  
* Players improve individual contribution (e.g., decurses, uptime, efficiency)  
* Guilds reduce clear times and increase consistency  
* Leadership gains clarity around contribution-based reward systems

### **Business / Product Metrics**

* **Primary success metric:** number of guilds consistently uploading logs  
* Secondary metrics:  
  * Guild page views  
  * Engagement with advanced insights  
  * Qualitative feedback via Discord or direct channels

## **5. Core Features (Launch Scope)**

### **Data Input**

* Manual log uploads  
* Technical requirements clearly documented  
* Archive of previous uploads per guild

### **Supported Content (Initial)**

* **Raids:** Molten Core, Onyxia  
* **Dungeons:** Wailing Caverns, Deadmines, Ragefire Chasm

### **Core Metrics**

* Damage done  
* Healing done/Overhealing  
  Consumables used (including inferred monetary investment)

### **Feedback & Insight**

* Optional, discoverable improvement insights  
* Analytical and neutral tone  
* Designed for leaders seeking deeper understanding, not surface-level judgment

## **6. Non-Goals (Initial)**

* No live resource tracking (mana/health)  
* No player positioning replay  
  No automated upload tooling at launch  
* No privacy controls (guild pages are public by default)

These are acknowledged future possibilities but intentionally excluded to preserve focus and stability.

## **7. Differentiation & Positioning**

### **Competitive Landscape**

* **Warcraft Logs** (reference model, not direct competitor)  
* **Turt Logs** (primary competitor)  
* Ad-hoc GitHub scripts (functional but inaccessible)

### **Chronicle's Core Difference**

*Chronicle provides a unified, accessible interface that makes raid data readable, contextual, and useful for leadership.*

Key differentiators:

* Lower learning curve  
* Broader performance context  
* Guild-centric architecture  
* Expandable analytical depth  
* Neutral, scholarly presentation

## **8. Brand Positioning**

### **Brand Attributes**

* Data-driven  
* Welcoming  
* Neutral and analytical

### **Emotional Goals**

* Raid leaders feel **empowered to lead**  
* Players feel **motivated to improve**  
* Guilds feel **confident in their contribution and progression**

### **Relationship to WoW Aesthetic**

* Subtle Classic WoW inspiration  
* Arcane library / chronicle-keeping visual language  
* Avoids direct lore references or copyrighted material

## **9. Tone & Voice Guidelines**

* Analytical, not judgmental  
* Neutral and factual  
* Clear, concise language  
* Avoids elitism and parse-culture language  
* Focuses on insight, not shaming

## **10. Website Scope & Information Architecture**

### **Core Pages**

* **Home** — Value proposition, credibility, beta framing  
* **How It Works** — Log requirements, technical setup, supported content  
* **Raid Overview Page** — Guild-level and raid-level insights  
* **Upload Page** — Manual upload with archive access  
* **Account Settings** — User management and preferences  
* **Contact / About** — Vision, transparency, feedback pathways

### **Guild Pages**

* Public by default  
* Designed as shareable progression artifacts  
* Serve both internal leadership and external visibility (e.g., recruitment)

## **11. Monetization**

* Free, donation-supported tool  
* No paywalls at launch  
* Future consideration for privacy or premium features tied to significant donations

## **12. Roadmap Awareness**

### **Near-Term**

* Expanded raid support  
* Deeper analytics  
* Improved feedback systems  
* Desktop upload companion (one-click solution)

### **Long-Term**

* Support for additional Classic servers  
* Brand remains Classic-aligned but server-agnostic

## **13. Constraints & Principles**

* Accessibility over exhaustiveness  
* Stability over feature breadth  
* Trust through transparency  
* Growth through community feedback  
* No compromise on data accuracy
